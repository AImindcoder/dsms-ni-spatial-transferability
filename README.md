# Spatial Transferability of Cenozoic Marine Sedimentary Nitrogen Isotope Records

Research materials associated with:

**Testing the Spatial Transferability of Cenozoic Marine Sedimentary Nitrogen Isotope Records: A Publication- and Sequence-Independent Machine-Learning Assessment**

## Authors

- Sahil Khan — UIN Maulana Malik Ibrahim Malang, Indonesia
- Mouad el Baz — Université Sultan Moulay Slimane, Morocco
- Qasim Khan — The Education University of Hong Kong

## Research overview

This study evaluates the spatial transferability of Cenozoic marine sedimentary nitrogen isotope records using machine-learning methods and spatially structured validation.

The manuscript describes:

- Spatially blocked evaluation
- Publication- and sequence-independent assessment
- An archived ExtraTrees experiment
- A separately implemented five-model benchmark
- Comparison against a training-median baseline
- Prediction-error and calibration analyses
- Additional nitrogen and organic-carbon predictor experiments

## Repository status

**Research materials under verification.**

The current repository is a preparation package, not yet a fully verified reproduction of all reported results.

In particular, the archived ExtraTrees experiment and the separately implemented five-model benchmark must not be treated as identical computational runs.
